# v0.4.8 - the registration experience, complete

The safe successor to v0.4.7 (which raced a retag while its AAB was
already being uploaded - this one carries the whole state, no
ambiguity):

- **The registration gate unblocked:** `FXCLOUD_REGISTER_BUDGET`
  (default stays 10, set it on a test deployment - carrier-grade NAT
  hands many testers one address) and app errors that show the
  server's own reason - "retry in 328 seconds" instead of "(429)".
- **Feedback you can see:** the account-created / activation hint
  moves above the login form (it used to sit below the fold of a
  column that neither scrolled nor fit), the column scrolls, and the
  copy tells testers what happens next: an administrator has to
  activate the account.
- **Passkey sign-in removed from the app** (provider-blocked on
  every path we could test; the record lives in the spec and help
  guide). The web keeps passkeys; the app signs in with password,
  fingerprint and auto-unlock.
- **Groups slice planned** in the spec (admin groundwork, group
  cards with roles and quota, the group-key path).

## Compatibility

No API, no data, no manifest change. The AAB ships as **versionCode
12** - an update over whatever the closed track holds; the 14-day
counter is untouched (it tracks tester opt-in, not builds).
