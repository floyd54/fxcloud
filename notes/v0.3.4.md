# v0.3.4 - the mark, again: light blue, big cloud, shadow

The first redesign drew complaints on the device: a small blue cloud
on black with a squeezed fx. This pass follows the brief exactly -
the whole field light blue (#D5EEFB -> #C5E5F8), the cloud solid
#0082c9 and40% larger so the white fx breathes, and a soft drop
shadow under the cloud. The Android launcher icon carries the same
mark (same three puffs, same field, same shadow).

## What changed

- **Icons**: all five PWA icons (32/192/512/maskable/180) regenerated
  by `frontend/tests/gen-icons.js` - the artwork lives in the script,
  the PNGs stay pinned against it.
- **Generator**: gradient field, solid cloud with a smooth SDF drop
  shadow (cloud lifted by DROP, edge over BLUR), CLOUD_SCALE on the
  cloud only - the fx keeps its size and gains the room.

## Compatibility

Icons only: no API, no JavaScript, no data, no manifest change. A
home-screen shortcut picks the new icon up with the next manifest
refresh; re-add the shortcut if the browser holds on to the old one.

## Tests

Suite stays at **879 checks**, including the pixel-level icon check;
`cargo test` stays at90. The honest verifier is a look at the tab
icon and the home-screen shortcut after this update.
