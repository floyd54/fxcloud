# v0.0.35 - the settings dialog fits the screen again: a nav with one pane at a time

v0.0.33 added the session list to the settings dialog, and with it the
dialog grew to seven stacked sections. On a normal laptop screen it
outran the viewport - cut off at the top and the bottom, with the last
rows out of reach and no way to scroll the modal itself. This release
rebuilds the dialog's layout. It is a user-interface fix and carries no
server or data changes.

## What changed

- **A navigation column on the left, one pane on the right.** The five
  nav points are Account, Appearance, Password, Sessions and Passkeys.
  Account holds the account facts and the display name; Appearance
  groups the theme switch and the language picker (which used to be two
  separate sections); Password, Sessions and Passkeys each stand on
  their own, so the panes that used to be the tallest are now the only
  thing in view when you open them.
- **The dialog can no longer outgrow the screen.** It is capped at the
  viewport and the content column scrolls inside it - a long session
  list scrolls in its pane instead of pushing the footer off-screen.
  Opening the dialog lands on Account; on screens narrower than 560px
  the nav turns horizontal and scrolls sideways.
- Everything the UI and the suite knew kept its identity: the session
  list, the passkey list, the theme picker, the status line and every
  translated label are the same elements they were, now inside panes.

## Why it matters

The session list is the incident tool of v0.0.33 - "which device is
this, and end it" - and a tool you cannot reach is not one. Grouping
the dialog also makes it navigable at a glance: five named doors
instead of one long scroll, and the theme/language pair - changed
together far more often than anything else - sits in one pane.

## Trade-offs, stated openly

- One pane at a time hides the other sections until you pick them; the
  nav keeps all five names visible, so nothing is lost, and opening on
  Account preserves the old first-glance content.
- The nav adds a click to reach, say, Passkeys directly from the top of
  the dialog. The alternative - a taller dialog - is the bug this
  release fixes.

## Action required

- Operators: none; the change ships inside the binary's embedded
  frontend.
- Users: hard-refresh the page (Ctrl+Shift+R) once after the update if
  the dialog still looks like the old stacked list - the browser may
  hold the previous app.js or stylesheet.
- This is also the first release whose installer verifies the download
  against the `SHA256SUMS` asset (F7): the update line reports
  "checksum verified against SHA256SUMS".
